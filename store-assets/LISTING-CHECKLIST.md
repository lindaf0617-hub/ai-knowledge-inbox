# Browser store listing checklist

- [ ] Store ZIP has `manifest.json` at its root and passes `scripts/package-validator.js`.
- [ ] Name, short/detailed descriptions, category, support URL, and privacy URL are current.
- [ ] `PERMISSIONS.md` rationale is copied into the store review notes.
- [ ] `PRIVACY.txt` is copied into the privacy disclosure.
- [ ] Upload 128 px icon and screenshots for capture, library, and cited Ask.
- [ ] Confirm screenshots contain no private knowledge or account information.
- [ ] Test install, capture, local fallback, pairing, sync, update check, and uninstall.
- [ ] Select the correct Stable or Prerelease release channel and retain the prior approved ZIP.
